Our goal shouldn't be to be a preacher, but we should be a living epistle in the place God has assigned for us. God has a better plan of us than what we perceive our path to be. Pastor Josh brings up an interesting fact in the story of Abraham going to sacrifice Isaac. Research tells us that Isaac was in is thirties when Abraham went to sacrifice him. That means that Isaac could've rebelled against Abraham and God during that instruction, but he allow the instruction to go on. Isaac had a different mindset regarding the will of God and his life. He knew what God said and followed it fully. We have to change our perception of God and our view of the Bible. We cannot follow our daily traditions of declarations or others and go about our own ways. There is so much that we don't know about God that we need to reshape our mindset to get there.
